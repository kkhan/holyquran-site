Privacy policy

Holy Quran app

Last updated: 24 November 2026

Holy Quran is a free app developed by Kamran Rasheed Qureshi. This page explains what the app stores, what it does not collect, and how you can delete your data. The app has no server of its own, and nothing about you is sent to us.

What we do not collect

The app has no ads, no analytics, and no crash-reporting service. It does not build a profile of you, and it does not sell or share your data. There is no account with us to create.

Data on your device

By default, your bookmarks, notes, reading place, reader settings, prayer-time settings and any files you download (translations, tafseer or recitations) stay on the device. The Quran text itself is bundled with the app and is never altered.

Backup file

If you use Export backup, the app writes a small file you can save or share yourself. That file contains bookmarks (with notes), your reading place and reader settings. It does not include downloaded audio, translations or tafseer, and it does not include your prayer-times location. You choose where that file goes.

Google Drive sync (optional)

On Android phones, tablets and Android TV / Google TV, you can turn on Sign in with Google to sync. This is optional. The app asks Google only for the drive.file scope, which lets it see files the app created, not the rest of your Drive.

The app only reads and writes a single file in your own Google Drive, named Holy Quran sync.json. That file holds bookmarks, your reading place and reader settings, so they can stay in step across your devices. It does not include downloaded audio, translations or tafseer, and it does not include your location.

On a TV, sign-in uses a code. You open google.com/device on a phone, enter the code, and approve access with your Google account.

Google provides the sign-in and Drive storage under Google’s own terms and privacy policy. We cannot see your Google account or the file in your Drive.

iCloud sync (optional, iPhone and iPad)

When this is available in the App Store version and you turn it on, the same kind of data (bookmarks, reading place and reader settings) can be kept in your own iCloud, on Apple’s service, so it stays in step across your Apple devices. We cannot see that data.

Android Auto Backup

On Android, if you have device backup turned on in your system settings, Android may include this app’s settings, bookmarks and reading place in the backup to your own Google account, so they can come back when you install the app on a new phone. Downloaded recitations, translations and tafseer are left out of that backup.

Location

Location is optional and used only on the device, to calculate prayer times and the Qibla direction. You can pick a city instead. If you allow a location fix, the coordinates are saved on the device. They are not sent to us (we have no server), and they are not included in Google Drive sync, iCloud sync or the backup file.

Optional downloads

If you download recitations, translations or tafseer, those files come straight from their publishers to your device (for example mp3quran.net, QuranEnc, Tanzil and the spa5k tafseer collection). Those sites may see a normal download request from your device, as with any browser. After a download, the file is used offline.

Notifications

Optional daily reminders and prayer-time alerts are shown by the device itself. They are not sent through a server of ours.

How to delete your data

  • To stop Google Drive sync, turn off Sign in with Google to sync in About › Backup & restore. Then delete the Holy Quran sync.json file from your Google Drive if you no longer want that copy.
  • To stop iCloud sync, turn it off in the app. You can also remove the app’s iCloud data from your Apple ID settings.
  • Uninstalling the app removes the copy on that device, including bookmarks, settings and downloads stored there.
  • A backup file you exported is under your control: delete it wherever you saved it.

Children

The app does not collect personal information. A parent can use it without creating an account with us.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated text will be published on this page with a new date.
