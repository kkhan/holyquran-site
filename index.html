Holy Quran

A free app for phone, tablet and TV

Holy Quran is a free app for reading the Quran. Every feature is included. There are no ads, no purchases, and no account you have to create. The app has no server of its own, and nothing about you is sent to us.

The Quran text is from the Tanzil Project. It is used verbatim and is never altered.

What it does

  • Read the Quran offline, with translations, tafseer, search, bookmarks and recitation.
  • Optional prayer times and Qibla, calculated on your device from a city you pick or from your location.
  • Your bookmarks, reading place and settings stay on the device unless you choose to back them up or turn on sync.

Backup and sync

You can export a small backup file and import it on another phone or tablet (iPhone, iPad or Android).

If you turn on Google Drive sync (Android and Android TV), the app only reads and writes one file it created in your own Drive, named Holy Quran sync.json. It does not see the rest of your Drive. On a TV, you approve sign-in on a phone with a code at google.com/device.

On iPhone and iPad, an optional iCloud sync is planned for the App Store version. Until then, use a backup file.

Privacy

The app has no ads and no analytics. Please read the privacy policy and the terms of service for the details, including location, how to delete your data, and how Google Drive sync works.
